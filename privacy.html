CLI (Doc Bridge) — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

CLI is a personal command-line tool used by its owner to read and write their own Google Docs and Drive metadata. It is not distributed to third parties.

Data accessed

The tool requests the drive and documents scopes solely to read a document's text and append replies to it. No other files are accessed.

Data storage

OAuth credentials are stored locally on the owner's computer in encrypted form. Document contents are not retained by the tool beyond local conversation logs and state files on the owner's computer.

Data sharing

No data is sold, shared, or transferred to third parties. Content is processed only by Google APIs and by the owner's own AI model provider when generating a reply.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
